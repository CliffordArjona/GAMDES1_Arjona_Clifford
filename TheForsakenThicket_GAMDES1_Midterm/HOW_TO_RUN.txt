THE FORSAKEN THICKET  -  Windows Executable

1. Extract TheForsakenThicket.exe.zip
2. Double-click TheForsakenThicket.exe  (no installation needed).
3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway"
   (the game is not code-signed, which is normal for student projects).

Controls: W A S D move | Space / left click sword | Q / right click Mana Bolt | Shift or Ctrl dodge |
E or F interact | 1 / 2 (or Z / X) potions | Tab or C status | Esc or P pause (Save Game is in there) |
F11 fullscreen
Saves are stored in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\The Forsaken Thicket\saves
